Overview

"Kiini" is Swahili for core — the center everything else is built around. That's the idea the whole system runs on: one core, every part of the business connected to it.

Kiini brings the business into one connected workspace, giving teams clarity and control across the work they do every day.

Logomark

Concept

Two concentric rings converge on a solid core. The rings read as the hub — connected modules, data, and teams — orbiting a single point of control. The solid center is deliberately the warmest color in the mark: it's where control lives.

Clear spaceKeep space equal to the mark's radius on all sides. Never crop the outer ring.
Minimum size24px for the mark alone, 120px width for the full lockup with wordmark.
Don'tRecolor the core off-amber, stretch the rings unevenly, or add a drop shadow.
Don'tPlace the mark on busy photography without a solid backing shape.

Color

Indigo carries the brand's weight; amber marks the single point of control; teal is the connective, growth color. Together they read as grounded and precise rather than loud.

Indigo and Paper are the two backgrounds (dark UI / light marketing pages). Amber is reserved for the logomark core, primary actions, and the one accent per screen — never for large fills or body text.

Typography

Headlines · Manrope 800
Every department, one core.
Body · Manrope 400/500
Kiini connects CRM, operations, and reporting into a single system, so nothing runs on a spreadsheet no one else can see.
Editorial accent · Newsreader italic
Used sparingly — pull quotes, the tagline in long-form contexts, nothing in the product UI.

Voice

DirectState what the product does, not what it "empowers" you to do. Say "Kiini tracks every invoice", not "Kiini empowers seamless financial visibility."
GroundedWrite for a business owner running real operations, not a hype cycle. No "revolutionize," "game-changing," "seamless."
PreciseName the actual module or action. "Approve the invoice," not "take control of your workflow."
Do"One hub for sales, stock, and staff — no more switching tabs."
Don't"Unlock unparalleled synergy across your entire business ecosystem."
