Platform Overview

Kiini is an organization-aware business operations platform covering customer management, finance, projects, people, procurement, reporting, and administration — one core system for every part of the business.

CapabilityCurrent state
Role-based navigationAvailable
Organization-scoped recordsAvailable
Notifications and activity historyAvailable
