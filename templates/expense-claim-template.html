Expense Claim Terms & Conditions

  1. Claim Submission: All claims must be submitted within 30 days of expense incurrence with complete supporting documentation.
  2. Eligible Expenses: Only business-related expenses as per company policy are eligible for reimbursement.
  3. Documentation: Original receipts, invoices, or payment evidence must accompany all claims.
  4. Approval Required: Claims must be approved by employee's manager and finance team before reimbursement.
  5. Processing Time: Approved claims will be processed within 7-10 business days.
  6. Advance Deduction: Any previous advances will be deducted from the reimbursable amount.
  7. False Claims: Submission of fraudulent claims may result in disciplinary action.
  8. Tax Compliance: Expenses will be categorized for tax compliance and reporting purposes.
